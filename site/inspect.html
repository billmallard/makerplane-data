Experimental / Amateur-Built use only. Not certified. This data is provided as-is with no warranty and any use of the data is at your own risk.

The page fetches manifest.json and its signature, verifies the signature against the published key (keys/minisign.pub) with your browser's own Ed25519, then downloads the pack and checks its size and SHA-256 against the signed entry. Only then does SQLite (sql.js, loaded by hash from cdnjs) open it, with writes switched off. Links that open a place in a pack: pack_inspector.md.
